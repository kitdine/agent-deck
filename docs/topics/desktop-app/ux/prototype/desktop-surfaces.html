Provider switching · the ⌄ footer menu

Demoted on purpose. It is a rare, deliberate action, and it has no business competing with the numbers a user opens this for.

Widgets

Four content-typed widgets rather than one generic card. A widget that shows a single number is a worse menu bar item.

What changed after review, and why. The first board drew a title bar with a close button, put Settings and Quit in the reading surface, and led with provider switching. A MenuBarExtra popover is dismissed by clicking away — the chrome does not exist, and inventing it was a platform error, not a style choice. Switching is a rare deliberate act; it now lives in the footer menu beside Settings and Quit, where a rare act belongs.
What this forces upstream. Trend, models, cache-hit and attribution are not in the App Group projection architecture.md currently permits, and the widget document rejected them on that basis — treating a contract I wrote as if it were a law of nature. The projection must gain a bounded daily series, top-N model shares, and attribution counts. None of it is sensitive: model names and daily token totals carry no session content, no paths, and no credentials.
What a specimen cannot settle. Hierarchy, density, copy, proportion at both widths, and both appearances are reviewable here. Real SF type metrics, Dynamic Type reflow, VoiceOver order, and measured contrast are runtime claims and stay in the manual acceptance checklist.
